## 🎯 The HF Analyst Role

What hedge fund analysts actually do, based on 720 job descriptions.

| Guide | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| [My vision of the role](role/01-my-vision.md) | How I see the hedge fund analyst role, buy-side vs sell-side, the idea-to-portfolio lifecycle |
| [Skills analysis](role/02-skills.md) | Top buy-side skills, investment strategies, analytical platforms, frameworks like variant perception and catalyst identification |
| [Responsibilities](role/03-responsibilities.md) | Patterns extracted from 2,800+ job responsibilities |

89.2% of buy-side roles require skills beyond stock picking. Financial modeling is mandatory in 78.4% of roles. Only 5.4% are pure quantitative - the market wants analysts who can build conviction and communicate investment theses.
